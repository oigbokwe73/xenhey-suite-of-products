## Treasury-services UI

Use service cards:

* ACH payments
* Domestic and international wires
* Bill pay
* Positive Pay
* Remote deposit capture
* Account alerts
* Sweep services
* Payroll integration

Each card should display whether the service is available, requested, under review, enabled or suspended.

## Debit-card management UI

Provide:

* Cardholder name
* Masked card number
* Current status
* Daily spending limit
* ATM access
* International usage
* Recent transactions
* Lock or unlock card
* Report lost card
* Request replacement

## Recommended Xenhey dashboard structure

For your Business Checking solution, divide the experience into two states:

**Before account activation**

* Application progress
* Missing information
* Documents requested
* Bank-review status
* Continue Application
* Contact assigned representative

**After account activation**

* Account balances
* Transactions
* Transfers and payments
* Approval requests
* Treasury services
* Debit cards
* Statements and reports
* Alerts and support

This would extend your current [Xenhey Business Checking UI](https://www.xenhey.com/api/store/E15F2B19B128485497B07997600D9F85) from an account-opening portal into a complete business-banking workspace.
